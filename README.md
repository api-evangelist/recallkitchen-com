Recall Kitchen is an Iowa‑based company that aggregates U.S. product recall alerts from government agencies such as the FDA, USDA, CPSC, and NHTSA. Users can create watchlists, receive free email alerts, and search recalls by product name, lot code, or UPC. The service is free, with optional paid plans for advanced features, and aims to help consumers, restaurants, and retailers stay informed about safety notices.
